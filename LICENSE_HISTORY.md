Rights already granted under AGPL for those historical revisions remain governed by the AGPL terms that accompanied those revisions. The current license change does not revoke, narrow, or replace rights already granted for historical AGPL-covered copies.

The exact AGPL license text remains available in Git history and the `v0.51.0` release/tag.

## Current Community Source line

Beginning with the license-migration revision after commit `6600da2899d83eddcfc43efbc2d81805d662d77a`, the current BossAI Funding source line is offered under **BossAI Community Source License 1.0** unless a file or third-party notice states otherwise.

The Community Source license permits personal, educational, evaluation, research, and other non-commercial use. Commercial use of a Community Source revision requires BossAI commercial authorization.
