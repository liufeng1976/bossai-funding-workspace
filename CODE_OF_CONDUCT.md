# Code of Conduct

BossAI Funding welcomes technical discussion, bug reports, product feedback and contributions from people who participate constructively.

## Expected behavior

- discuss ideas and code on their merits;
- give reproducible evidence when reporting problems;
- respect privacy, confidential information and security boundaries;
- disagree without personal attacks, harassment, threats or discrimination;
- do not spam, impersonate others, manipulate community signals or knowingly submit plagiarized / incompatible code;
- disclose conflicts of interest that materially affect a review.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, doxxing, threats, deliberate disclosure of secrets or vulnerabilities, and repeated disruption of project discussion are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove contributions and discussions that violate this policy, and may restrict participation for serious or repeated violations.

Security reports should follow `SECURITY.md`, not public issue discussion.

This policy governs participation in the BossAI Funding project; it does not grant trademark, commercial-license, support or employment rights.
