# BossAI Funding · CEO Legal Review Waiver

Date: 2026-08-20
Decision authority: CEO / repository owner

## Decision

For BossAI Funding source publication, qualified outside/legal-counsel review of the dual-license structure and contributor-rights documents is explicitly waived by the CEO.

This decision means:

- lack of outside-counsel review is no longer a blocker to changing the GitHub repository from Private to Public;
- no document, checklist, release note, repository metadata, or product claim may state or imply that a lawyer approved the licensing or CLA;
- AGPL-3.0-or-later remains the canonical open-source license;
- the separate commercial-license path remains an alternative permission path and must not restrict rights already granted by the AGPL;
- the CLA / contributor-rights workflow remains fail-closed until an operational contributor-signing mechanism exists; external contributions must not be merged merely because legal review was waived;
- public source publication remains separate from an official signed Windows release and from production proprietary-commercial acceptance.

## Remaining non-legal blockers

The waiver does not approve or waive:

- publicly trusted Windows code signing;
- official BossAI Funding icon / release-brand metadata;
- a real production Headquarters Commerce plan/account granting `bossai-funding.commercial`;
- production paid-account commercial E2E acceptance;
- production identity/security readiness for remote operation.
